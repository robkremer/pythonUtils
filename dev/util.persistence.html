util.persistence package¶

Persistence: databases of objects that are saved to and read from files (or URLs), and refer to one another – across files – by reference:

  • util.persistence.pRef: the databases (PRDb), their objects (PRObject) and references to them (PReference).

  • util.persistence.serialize: how objects describe themselves for saving (InstantiationInfo), and the SerializationStrategy that writes and reads them – in XML (serializeXML, and the more compact serializeXML2), JSON (serializeJSON) or property lists (serializePlist).
